# Capstone Project: Customer Data Analysis

# Dataset (Input)
The dataset contains shopping information from 10 different shopping malls. Data is from various age groups and genders to provide a comprehensive view of shopping habits.

# Insights (Output)
• The shopping distribution by gender are 620 customers are female and 
380 are male, with female customers representing the larger share of the 
shopping mall's customer base. 

• The total quantity of products sold was 1853 to female customers and 
1128 to male customers, indicating higher product sales among female 
customers. 

• Female customers generated 60% of the total revenue, while male 
customers 39%, indicating that female customers generate a significantly 
higher share of revenue than male customers. 

• The highest number of purchases, accounting for 517 customers, is 
observed for products priced below 250 TL, indicating a strong customer 
preference for lower-priced products. Customers are more inclined to 
purchase affordable products. 

• The 36–45 age category accounts for the highest number of customer 
visits to the mall compared with all other age groups. 

• Analysis of the sales data reveals that the 36-45 age group recorded the 
highest volume of product purchases, highlighting their strong purchasing 
power. 

• Although the 36-45 age category recorded the highest volume of product 
purchases, the 56-65 age category generated the highest revenue, 
indicating that customers with this age category making higher-value 
transactions and contribute more significantly to overall revenue. 

• The majority of customers prefer cash as their primary payment method, 
resulting in relatively lower usage of debit card or credit card. Therefore, 
offering discounts exclusively on debit card transactions may not be the 
most effective strategy for increasing sales or influencing customer 
purchasing behaviour.
